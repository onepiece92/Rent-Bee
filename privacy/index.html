Privacy Policy for Rent Bee

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Rent Bee is a rent ledger app for landlords, operated by BrandBuilder Pvt Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

1. Information the app handles

Your email address or phone number. You sign in with a Google account, with Apple, or with your mobile number. Google Firebase Authentication receives the email address that Google or Apple shares (with Apple you can choose to hide your real address), or your mobile number so it can deliver a one-time SMS code. We use it only to recognise your account when you sign in again. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

Your rent ledger. The information you enter in the app:

  • units and tenants: unit code, tenant name, business type, tenant phone number, rent start date and your notes
  • money records: monthly rent, payments, payment method and notes, security deposits and refunds
  • monthly charges: electricity, water and other charges, and deductions with their notes
  • your app settings: calendar (Bikram Sambat or Gregorian), currency and annual rent increase

A security check. The app uses Firebase App Check, which confirms to Google that requests come from a genuine copy of Rent Bee. It does not tell us who you are.

Your PIN is stored only on your device, in protected form. It is never sent to us.

2. Information the app does not collect

  • No analytics, advertising identifiers or crash reports.
  • No location.
  • No access to your address book. If you choose "pick from contacts" when adding a tenant, only the one contact you select is copied into the form.
  • No photos or camera images. The app asks for a file only when you choose to import or restore your own data.
  • The app does not send SMS by itself. Rent reminders open your phone's Messages app with a draft that you choose to send.

3. Where your information is stored

Your ledger is stored on your device so the app works without internet. After you sign in, it is also backed up automatically to Google Cloud Firestore, under an account only you can read, so that you can restore it and use it on another device. Google stores this data on its servers, which may be outside your country.

We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing. Our service provider is Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and App Check), which processes the data on our behalf.

4. Tenant information

The tenant names and phone numbers you enter are stored as part of your ledger and backed up as described above. You are responsible for having the right to record your tenants' details.

5. Keeping, exporting and deleting your data

  • Export. Settings lets you export your ledger as CSV or a full backup file at any time. The file goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Erase. "Erase all data" in Settings deletes your units, payments and charges from your device and from the cloud backup.
  • Delete your account. To delete your account and any remaining data, message us on WhatsApp at +977 9802850777, stating the email address or phone number you signed in with. We delete the account and its data within 30 days.
  • Sign out removes your sign-in and PIN from the device. Your ledger stays on the device and in your cloud backup until you erase it.

We keep your data for as long as your account exists.

6. Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and Google's servers, and each account's cloud data can be read only by that signed-in account. A PIN protects the app on your device. No method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Rent Bee is intended for adults managing rental property and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.

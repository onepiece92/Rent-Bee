Delete your Rent Bee account

Rent Bee: Rent Tracker is made by BrandBuilder Pvt Ltd. This page explains how to delete your Rent Bee account and its data, and what is deleted.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Rent Bee and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings > Security > Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE, then sign in once more to confirm it is you.

Your account and all of its data are deleted straight away.

This option is in Rent Bee 1.3.0 and later. If you do not see it, update the app or use the option below.

Can't open the app?

Message us on WhatsApp at +977 9802850777 with "Delete my Rent Bee account" and the phone number or Google/Apple email address you signed in with. We delete your account and all of its data within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account: your phone number or email address, your Google profile name, and any Google or Apple sign-in link (Apple access is revoked)
  • Your settings: calendar, currency and annual rent increase
  • Your whole ledger: units, tenants, payments, charges, deductions and deposits, both in the cloud and on your device

What is kept

  • We keep no copy of your account or your ledger.
  • Files you exported yourself (CSV or backup files) are yours and stay wherever you saved them.
  • Google's own backups of Firebase data are cleared on Google's schedule, under Firebase's data deletion policy.

Delete your records but keep your account

Use Erase all data in the app's Settings. It clears your ledger in the cloud and on every device you are signed in on. Your account and settings stay, so you can start a fresh ledger.

Want to keep your records first? Export a CSV or backup file from Settings before you delete your account.
